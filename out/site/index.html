A census of vocabularies embedded in English printed books, 1473–1700

Wordes of Their Language

Travellers, missionaries and compilers printed thousands of words from languages they met, usually as a short list tucked inside a voyage account. This catalogue finds those lists in the 60,329 transcribed books of EEBO-TCP, keeps every form exactly as printed, and tests each list against modern lexical databases to ask which language it actually records, and where it was copied from.
