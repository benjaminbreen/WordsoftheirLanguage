Dear colleagues,

I am a historian at the University of California, Santa Cruz, working on early records of the
languages of Australia and the Pacific. I would like to request digital images of the whole of
GBR/0374/BFBS/BSMS 338, "Vocabulary, 1800–1825" (ff. I + 39 + I).

Your catalogue records a note on ff. 26v–27r saying that the "parallel words in the dialect of
Botany Bay or Sidney Town" were supplied by the Rev. Samuel Marsden. As far as I can tell this
manuscript has not been described in the literature on the Sydney language, which survives in
very few sources, so the Sydney column could be of real value to linguists and to Dharug
language revival.

Could you let me know whether images can be supplied (or whether the volume could be digitised
for Cambridge Digital Library), and the cost and reproduction terms? Low or medium resolution
images would be sufficient for transcription.
