Subject: Enquiry: Marsden manuscripts (Palauan, Shilha, d'Entrecasteaux vocabularies)

Dear colleagues,

I am a historian at the University of California, Santa Cruz, working on early vocabularies of
non-European languages. William Marsden's Bibliotheca Marsdeniana (1827, pp. 301–308) lists
several manuscript vocabularies that I believe passed to SOAS with the Marsden Collection, and I
would be grateful to know their present shelfmarks and whether images could be supplied:

1. "Vocabulary of the language of the Pelew islands, with Malayan and English" (4to).
2. An English–"Mauritanian" (Moroccan Arabic)–Shilha vocabulary in Maghribi script, obtained
   through Consul J. M. Matra at Tangier, 1788.
3. Manuscript vocabularies of Van Diemen's Land and Tongatabu from the d'Entrecasteaux
   expedition (1793), and a Tongan vocabulary by an officer of L'Espérance.
4. "Short Vocabularies of the dialects of Asam".

I am also interested in any record of how the New South Wales vocabularies now at MS 41645
reached Marsden.

With thanks,
