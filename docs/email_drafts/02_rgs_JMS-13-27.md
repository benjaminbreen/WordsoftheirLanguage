Subject: Image request: JMS/13/27 (Vallack, Port Essington vocabulary, 1840)

Dear colleagues,

I am a historian at the University of California, Santa Cruz, working on early records of
Australian languages. I would like to request images of JMS/13/27, Mate Vallack's "vocabulary
of the Port Essington dialect" (1840, 9 pp.), sent to the Society by Captain Owen Stanley of
HMS Britomart (with the related letter RGS/CB2/506).

The catalogue notes that the vocabulary was recommended for publication, but I have found no
printed version, and it does not appear among the known early sources for the languages of the
Cobourg Peninsula. Could you tell me whether images can be supplied, and at what cost and on what
terms?
