# Gallica log (gallica)
- SRU access: requests sent with curl's default user agent got 403; requests with a browser-style user agent that names the research purpose went through. Page text (`.texteBrut`) is behind an ALTCHA security check, which I did not bypass. IIIF images and the OAIRecord/ContentSearch services work.
- I used the SRU `gallica all` / `gallica adj` queries with date < 1850: "vocabulaire de la langue (des)", "petit vocabulaire", "langue créole", "mots de la langue des", "manière de compter"+sauvages, "vocabulaire galibi", "langue malgache"+vocabulaire, "langue des nègres", "langue franque"+vocabulaire, vocabulaire+Casamance/Nossi-Bé/Mayotte/Grand-Bassam, Annales maritimes / Revue coloniale + vocabulaire.
- Full-text hits on printed works are dominated by encyclopedias and newspapers, and periodical volumes cannot be checked page by page without texteBrut. Searching the manuscript collection by title (dc.type manuscrit + vocabulaire/dictionnaire) was more productive.
- Manuscript searches: series "Africain: présentation des collections en langues africaines" (Africain 1 Peul by Caillé 1836, Africain 4, Africain 5 Congo dictionary, Africain 6 Abouré); Indien 731 (Aussant 1782); Américain 17, Américain 72.
- Not followed up: Africain 1 (Peul, well documented); Africain 5 (Congo dictionary, not checked); d'Abbadie notebooks (known); Kaqchikel and K'iche' vocabularies (19th c., Mesoamerican, outside focus); Rochefort's Carib vocabulary (known).
