# marsden log
Sources: IA bibliothecamars00marsgoog and b33521025 (Bibl. Marsdeniana 1827; MSS section pp.301-308, Malayan pages garbled in first copy, read from second); IA bim_eighteenth-century_a-catalogue-of-dictionar_marsden-william_1796 (printed only).
Parsed: 36 MS vocab/grammar items (1827) + 25 embedded vocabularies in travel books (1796; regex-captured, incomplete; ~264 "P." sublines exist; the remaining standalone dictionaries are mostly well-known printed works, not processed).
Glottolog: 5 yes, 12 no, 44 unclear (anonymous or unmapped).
1827 MSS have no stated provenance for the NSW items. Marsden 1834 "On the Polynesian" not found on IA; Sydney-word source not checked.
SOAS/KCL catalogues not queried; present locations marked unverified except Dawes/anon NSW notebooks (SOAS MS 41645, from literature).
Malayan section MSS (romances, tracts) and non-vocab MSS excluded.
