# Literature check: Stein van Gollenesse's "Mallabaars woordenboek" (1743), VOC inv. 2601 fol. 161-191

Checked 2026-10-04 with web search and fetch only. I did not consult any books in print.

## Verdict
**Apparently unpublished as a text. It has been used in passing by at least one scholar.** I found no edition, transcription or translation. I found one scholarly citation that quotes an entry from it.

## Evidence
1. **Scholarly use (positive evidence).** J.B. Mailaparambil, *The Ali Rajas of Cannanore: status and identity at the interface of commercial and political expansion, 1663-1723* (PhD thesis, Leiden, 2007), ch. "The Rajas of Kolathunadu", p. 55, n. 122: "VOC 2601, 'Mallabaarse Woorden Boeken', fo. 161r."
   - It quotes in English the Ali Raja origin story (a Kolathiri princess and an Arab or "Moor"; the title "Crau"), which is evidently the glossary's entry on the Ali Raja.
   - The same chapter also quotes the 1743 memoir itself via Galletti.
   - URL: https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item:2959806/view
   - The thesis was revised as *Lords of the Sea* (Brill, 2012). That book probably repeats the citation, but I have not verified this.
   - This is only a single quotation. It is not an edition or a study of the glossary.
2. **s'Jacob 1976** (*De Nederlanders in Kerala 1663-1701*, RGP Kleine serie 43). The title gives its coverage as 1663-1701, so it cannot include the 1743 memoir. I found no later RGP volume for Malabar.
   - s'Jacob's planned monograph on Cochin state formation, 1663-1730, drafted at NIAS in 1992-93, does not appear to have been published, and it would end before 1743 in any case.
   - Sources: https://isidore.science/document/10670/1.4914eb1f9e27f63c568c99702d6965f469286ca3 and https://nias.knaw.nl/?p=7262
3. **Madras/Chennai copy.**
   - The TANAP version of the Madras Record Office press list (1916/1952) has vol. 371, "Memoir. 1743", noted as printed as Dutch Records No. 1 and translated in No. 13. It records no glossary or attachment.
   - The press list has no entries for a glossary or word list anywhere in the Cochin records, going by a text search for memoir, woord, glossary and vocab.
   - The volume was still catalogued in the TANAP/GLOBALISE inventory, which reflects the 2000s Dutch Records Project.
   - URL: https://docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/tanap/archival-inventories/pdf/Chennai_DutchRecordsPresslist.pdf
   - This fits the glossary having been absent from the Madras copy from the start. That is my inference; I have not seen the volume.
4. **Searches that found nothing:**
   - the exact phrases "Mallabaarse Woorden", "Malabaarse woorden" and "Mallabaars woordenboek"
   - "VOC 2601" together with Malabar
   - Gollenesse with glossary, woordenboek or Siersma
   - Dutch loanwords and Malayalam lexicography with VOC
   - No results connected the glossary to Malayalam or Dutch lexicography.
   - "Found nothing" here is not proof of absence. Snippet search cannot see into Koshy 1989, Singh 2010, de Lannoy 1997, Malekandathil, Kurup, Poonen, Jha, or Malayalam translations of the Dutch records. None of these is available online as full text, and I did not check them individually.

## Remaining gaps

- Check the KCHR and Kerala State Archives Dutch-records translation series.
